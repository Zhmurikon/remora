Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity responsive product-design concept board for Remora web app
Primary request: Create a landscape concept board for the “Учебный радар” redesign of Remora’s unified materials library. Show one large desktop web-app viewport on the left and one complete mobile responsive viewport on the right, both light theme, no device mockup and no decorative presentation frame. This is an operating interface, not a marketing page.
Reference image role: carry over Remora’s warm ivory paper ground, deep forest text, jade/teal controls, restrained coral/ochre status accents, editorial heading character, subtle dotted/leaf motifs, and calm flat component style. Do not copy the reference screen’s content or phone hardware.
Desktop structure: persistent left navigation with Remora logo and items “Главная”, “Материалы” selected, “Практика”, “Достижения”, “Настройки”. Main title “Материалы” with primary button “Создать”. Directly below, a prominent but compact “Сегодня” attention queue with three rows: “Линейная алгебра — 18 повторить”, “Git и три зоны — давно не учились”, “Python для анализа данных — всё по плану”. Then tabs “Все”, “Курсы”, “Наборы”, “Сохранённые”; search and sort controls. Below, a clean scrollable list/grid of mixed courses and sets. Every material has title, type, card count, progress bar with text such as “Освоено 64%”, and an urgency phrase such as “18 повторить сегодня”, “Вернитесь на этой неделе”, or “Всё по плану”. Make weak materials visually easy to spot through a slim coral left rail and explicit text, not color alone. Include one saved item with author and “Есть обновление”. Use realistic Russian study content.
Mobile structure: same information hierarchy compressed into a top app bar, horizontally scrollable tabs, one compact “Сегодня” card, and single-column material rows; bottom navigation with “Главная”, “Материалы”, “Практика”, “Настройки”. Preserve 44px-scale tap targets.
Composition: 16:9 wide board; desktop about 70% width, mobile about 25%; enough vertical content to prove scroll rhythm. Pixel-sharp, production-grade UI, readable Russian labels, coherent spacing.
Constraints: One dominant primary action. Progress never communicated by color alone. No gradients, no glassmorphism, no oversized dashboard KPI cards, no fake charts, no English interface text, no lorem ipsum, no watermark.
