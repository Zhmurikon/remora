## А. Три образца

A — MATERIAL SAMPLES / horizontal triptych. Heading aligned to left margin. Three airy parallel equal-width columns without boxes and without arrows. Each opens with a different exquisitely detailed cobalt ballpoint sample on small creamy paper: a horizontal recorded audio waveform with a little play triangle and a transcript slip; two overlapping scraps of a grid of word pairs; one handmade index card with an anatomically drawn heart in ink and pale yellow masking tape. Drawings at common visual scale and baseline with plenty of negative space; left-align three corresponding source titles and body paragraphs below each exhibit. Anchor the register button below the columns at left. A student natural science field guide meets a calm modern web page. No horizontal connector so clearly alternatives.

## Б. Учебный стол

B — TWO-COLUMN WORKTABLE. Heading aligned upper left. Under it the LEFT half contains a singular carefully art-directed overhead product still life on a subtle pale-blue lightly squared drafting mat with a soft corner radius. Objects form one unified composition with beautiful natural contact shadows: cream index cards, blue metal binder clip, a slim audio waveform recording slip, a ruled two-column word sheet with only short handwritten word pairs, a pen drawing of an anatomical heart in blue ink, capped fountain pen. Right half is a clean vertical editorial list of the three sources with text: each source heading and corresponding body in full visible; thin light blue horizontal rule between methods. Button beneath right text aligned left. Premium, understated. No visual graphs, arrows, tabs or steps.

## В. Сравнение способов

C — EDITORIAL REFERENCE TABLE, a calm scholarly typesetter's interpretation like a well-designed literary almanac, pure white paper. Make an asymmetric layout radically different from three tiles. Left rail is 32 percent width, title wraps elegantly over 2 or 3 lines, generous empty space then the single CTA below it; small engraved blue pen-and-ink upright tray of cream blank index cards beneath the CTA gives weight. On the right occupies 63 percent, a single full-height reading table with EXACTLY THREE thin cobalt horizontal rules and three broad horizontal rows. In each row left column narrow: bold source name, then tariff on a second line in blue; middle column wide: regular body copy; far-right tiny discrete cobalt ink icon, in order a waveform, a two-column grid, a pencil. Row heights auto, no extra text headers, no row numbers, no duplicate headings, no outer boundary and no vertical lines. All three rows are peers, very breathable, print-inspired. Preserve full exact text. No large background rectangle.
