## Общий запрос

Generate ONE finished high fidelity 3:2 landscape website section design, NOT multiple layouts or a presentation. Desktop 1440 CSS px screenshot for Russian study platform Запоминальня. Heading in calm regular Georgia serif around 28 CSS px, method labels Inter semibold 22px, body Inter regular 16/25 px. White or warm paper, black-navy text, cobalt ink, yellow paper details. The whole copy is important; choose sufficient space, all the letters clearly typeset and large. Four ALTERNATIVE methods always visible, no repetition, no navigation, hero, numbering, decorative headlines, fake controls or invented text. Design must avoid the flaw of a tall empty left rail below an illustration. Left and right areas should end with balanced content. Use the following supplied copy VERBATIM, exactly once, including two paragraphs for the fourth method and 500 operations/week. At end of the 4th method place the underlined text link «Как подключить помощника». Put ONE primary button «Создать бесплатный аккаунт» in the designated location. Actual copy:
Как создать карточки по своим материалам

Из записи лекции · Plus и Pro

Загрузите аудио или видео с пары. Сначала вы получите расшифровку. По ней можно составить конспект, а из него — карточки. Перед сохранением поправьте термины и оставьте вопросы, которые преподаватель просил подготовить к зачёту.

Из таблицы · бесплатно

Скопируйте сразу два столбца, например английские слова и их переводы. Вставьте их в редактор набора. Перед сохранением вы увидите обе стороны каждой карточки.

Вручную · бесплатно

Запишите вопрос так, как его задаёт преподаватель, и добавьте ответ. В карточках можно использовать формулы, код и изображения. Например, на одну сторону поместите схему сердца с номерами, на другую — названия отделов под этими номерами.

С помощью своего ИИ

Попросите своего ИИ-помощника: «Составь 10 карточек по этому конспекту и сохрани их в Запоминальне». Помощник подготовит вопросы и ответы, а набор появится в вашей библиотеке.

Открытый API доступен бесплатно. Для помощников с поддержкой MCP есть готовый коннектор. На бесплатном тарифе — до 500 операций записи в неделю.

## Вариант А

Design A: Four paper specimens. Top header strip with title at left and the modest CTA at far right; below: snug 2×2 matrix of four distinct tactile paper folios laid flat, sharing a baseline. Eye order lecture upper left, spreadsheet upper right, manual lower left, AI lower right. No SaaS shadows or border boxes: light cream, plain white ruled, butter paper and subtle ice blue mix like genuine archival stationery. Little exquisite cobalt ballpoint vignettes nestled in folio corners (audio cassette strip, folded two-column glossary, anatomical ink diagram, two overlapping conversation slips). Give top row slightly less height, bottom row slightly more for AI, keep illustrations small in long texts. Body copy is crisp HTML-style typesetting separate from illustrations. A graphic designer has composed this four-panel grid carefully: handdrawn corner details visually balance the unequal paragraph lengths.

## Вариант Б

Design B: Wide open STUDY NOTEBOOK double-page spread, shot exactly overhead in a clean digital editorial style, no perspective distortion, visible fine soft binding seam at exact centre, warm-white pages with small cobalt bookcloth corners. Section title is outside and above the spread, aligned left. Left leaf contains the complete lecture method and complete table method, one under the other with small marginal cobalt pen vignette and subtle ruled baseline; CTA rests at foot of that leaf. Right leaf contains the manual method and the AI method, one under the other. AI prompt may be set as a single quoted line group marked with a fine hand-drawn blue bracket (do not duplicate it elsewhere); API quota and link at end. Both leaves read like a real art-directed study manual. Keep text printed upright, crisp, sans serif and fully legible, not handwriting. A subtle pale yellow bookmark near the fold, tiny ink illustration of a pencil on left counters remaining space. Avoid cards grid and gratuitous decoration.

## Вариант В

Design C: Digital editorial index with ONE wide cobalt-tinted bottom feature. Top line contains section title left. Under it, first three methods sit side-by-side in equal-width columns on pure white, divided only by fine vertical blue rules. Each column begins with a tiny finely etched blue icon next to the heading, body copy immediately below. Across the whole bottom width, a broad pale blue horizontal workspace strip dedicated to the fourth method. Split its interior in TWO clear halves: left half heading and the task example presented like a tasteful handwritten-margin speech slip (Cyrillic body still typeset), right half API explanation and underlined connection link. Place primary register CTA below the first three methods at the far right bottom EDGE of the entire composition aligned with the connection band. Add a small, finely hatched blue sketch of a stack of index cards if needed to balance, never a giant illustration. This is an editorial page diagram for a real product, premium modern newsroom polish. No bubbles for generic decoration, no oversized sidebar, no rounded tile grid.
